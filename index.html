◆ Projector Mapper

Shapes

    Pick a tool on the left and draw over the object you're projecting onto.

    Media

    Tap a thumbnail to put it on the selected shape, or on a new one if nothing is selected. You can also drag files onto a shape.

    Selected shape

    Perspective needs a shape with exactly 4 points. Corner order: ① top-left, ② top-right, ③ bottom-right, ④ bottom-left.

    Drag the dots to fit the shape to your object. Double-click an edge to add a point; right-click a point to remove it. Arrow keys nudge (Shift = 10×).

    Drag this window onto the projector, then double-click to go fullscreen.
